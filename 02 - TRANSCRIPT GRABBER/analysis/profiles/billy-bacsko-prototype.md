## Executive assessment

Billy Bacsko is a public illustrator, concept artist, comic-book colorist, studio operator, and recurring *Russian Troll Factory* panelist. Public professional pages connect him to Be Like Water Studios and OwlGhost Studios; comics coverage credits him on *The Goblin Girl* and a *FIENDISH: Chapter 2* variant cover; and the published 2025 *Fighting Fantasy Adventures* rulebook credits OwlGhost / Be Like Water Studios, naming Bacsko within its art team.

Within RTF, Billy has a recognizable production role. He is a recurring reader/performer and has delivered the closing found poem, but his absences are also a recurring joke and workflow problem: in February he is represented by a visual substitute, in July Piper takes the reading normally assigned to him, and the July 17 show does not introduce him. This combination suggests a real stable role without supporting the false claim that he attended every archived episode.

The strongest supported characterization is:

> A working visual-art professional and recurring RTF performer whose public footprint bridges commissioned/studio production, crowdfunded indie comics, and long-form ensemble commentary.

## Creative and professional history

### Visual-art identity

Bacsko's public professional description identifies him as an illustrator, concept artist, and comic-book colorist. His ArtStation work includes character-design and redesign exercises, while public project posts describe paint-over and coloring work. The breadth of labels suggests a production artist who can move between concept development, final rendering, comics, and studio deliverables.

### Studio work

Public portfolio metadata describes Bacsko as CEO of Be Like Water Studios and co-founder of OwlGhost Studios. Later social-profile language describes the OwlGhost role as former co-founder with art-direction and operations responsibilities.

Supported conclusions:

- Bacsko has publicly presented himself in both creative and operational roles.
- The studio identity is associated with illustration, game art, comics, and design.
- A published 2025 rulebook supplies independent evidence that the combined OwlGhost / Be Like Water Studios team delivered credited art.

Not established:

- present corporate ownership;
- dates or legal structure of either studio;
- revenue, employee relationships, or client contracts;
- which individual rulebook images were made by Bacsko.

### The Goblin Girl

The Splintering's October 2021 coverage of Dan Plegel's *The Goblin Girl* says the project features colors by Billy Bacsko. Later public posts credit Bacsko with full paint-over work on a wraparound cover based on Plegel's underdrawing.

Assessment: a substantive recurring creative credit rather than a mere promotional association.  
Confidence: High for the published color/cover credit.

### FIENDISH: Chapter 2

The *FIENDISH: Chapter 2: Origins* campaign credits a variant cover to Dan Plegel and Billy Bacsko. This supports cross-project collaboration in the crowdfunded fantasy-comics ecosystem.

### Fighting Fantasy Adventures

The 2025 rulebook credits art to several individual artists and to "OwlGhost / Be Like Water Studios," followed by Billy Bacsko, Jenner Chen, Jackson Milano, and Viktoriya Fajardo.

Assessment: strongest recovered formal publication credit outside campaign journalism.  
Confidence: High for team inclusion; individual asset attribution remains open.

### Livestream and discussion appearances

The Splintering records Bacsko joining its founder, comic writers, and other artists on an August 2022 *Sit DOWN Sunday* comics-news stream. Commi3 Mark's archive also records a *COMMI3CAST* installment featuring Billy Bacsko and *Team America: World Police*.

This shows an on-air history that predates the 2026 RTF sample and is not confined to one host.
