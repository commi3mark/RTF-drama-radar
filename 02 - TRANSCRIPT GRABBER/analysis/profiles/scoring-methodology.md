# Profile Scoring Methodology

The scores are comparative research aids for the current transcript archive. They are not objective measurements of real-world worth, power, dangerousness, guilt, intent, or moral character.

## ComicsGate/community power level — 0 to 10,000

The score combines archive appearances, total alias-matched mentions, distinct sources, attributable own-source episodes, episode-title prominence, and recency.

| Score | Band |
|---:|---|
| 9,000–10,000 | Central / system-shaping |
| 7,000–8,999 | Prominent |
| 5,000–6,999 | Established |
| 3,000–4,999 | Recurring |
| 1,000–2,999 | Peripheral but identifiable |
| 0–999 | Trace / insufficient corpus evidence |

## “Threat to Commi3 Mark” — public conflict/exposure risk — 0 to 10,000

This user-requested score is restricted to public-content risk. It combines Commi3-stream appearances and mentions, persistence across months, title prominence, conflict vocabulary in matching contexts, reciprocal references in attributable own-source episodes, and a small reach component.

It does not assess physical threat, criminal propensity, private intent, mental state, or moral character. Core panelists receive a collaboration adjustment, and Commi3 Mark’s self-risk score is zero.

| Score | Band |
|---:|---|
| 8,000–10,000 | Very high sustained public-conflict exposure |
| 6,000–7,999 | High public-conflict exposure |
| 4,000–5,999 | Moderate public-conflict exposure |
| 2,000–3,999 | Low public-conflict exposure |
| 0–1,999 | Minimal or insufficient evidence |

## Observable communication style

Style sections require material attributable to a matching source/channel. They measure caption vocabulary concerning evidence, uncertainty, assertiveness, conflict, humor, and questions.

These are lexical observations about public captions, not personality diagnoses. Played clips, guests, caption errors, and missing speaker labels can contaminate the measurements.

## Privacy

Profiles use public identities only. They must not reveal, connect, or speculate about separate legal identities or sensitive offline attributes.
