CROSS-SOURCE ANALYSIS
=====================

External reach:

The two Matt-centered reaction streams show that RTF content circulates beyond its own channel and can become the object of extended counter-commentary. This is evidence of network penetration, not audience scale. View counts and referral analytics would be required to quantify reach.

Relationship evidence:

Piper's song strengthens the “core operating circle” assessment. It indicates that her RTF involvement is not merely inferred from repeated appearances; she publicly narrates Commi3 as having recruited her.

Adversarial feedback:

Matt's response is important because it supplies right of reply to the 17 July RTF case. It does not erase the dated artifacts in the RTF report, but it requires disputed account ownership, project responsibility, and private-message claims to remain contested.

Duplicate-evidence control:

STANK and NQG both contain the same source artifact. Search systems must hash or otherwise identify replayed passages so that the same Commi3 monologue is not counted as:

- two independent descriptions;
- two independent Russian Troll Factory appearances;
- two separate confirmations of the Matt allegations.
