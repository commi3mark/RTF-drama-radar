==============

The episode is a practical demonstration of adversarial source checking. The Ministry account publishes a list of suspicious accounts and a multi-day guide for identifying bots or sock puppets. Commi3 accepts the observable premises—pseudonymity, communist imagery, high posting volume, location ambiguity, memes—but disputes the conclusion that these traits establish automation or covert coordination.

The decisive question is not whether the critic supplies links, but whether those links prove the caption placed around them. Commi3 repeatedly asks for an evidentiary bridge between “this account behaves unusually” and “this account is an automated or foreign-controlled operation.” The critic's failure to supply that bridge becomes the episode's central finding.

The episode also shows a strategic feature of Commi3's persona: hostile attention is converted into programme material. He withholds quote-tweet amplification while testing the account privately in-thread, then publicizes the exchange only after deciding he has won. That is controlled audience deployment, not proof of a bot network.
